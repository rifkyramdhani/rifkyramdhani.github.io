About Me

I'm a Data Engineer with over 3 years of professional experience developing ETL pipelines, optimizing SQL queries, and building Python-based automation to support business data operations. At Bina Nusantara IT Division, I work with relational databases and enterprise data platforms to improve data quality, streamline workflows, and maintain reliable data pipelines.

My professional and personal experience includes Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, Apache Airflow, SQL Server Integration Services (SSIS), SQL, Python, Snowflake, dbt, and Google BigQuery. Alongside my professional work, I build personal projects to deepen my expertise in modern data engineering, cloud data platforms, and workflow orchestration.

I'm passionate about designing reliable data solutions and continuously expanding my skills through hands-on projects and emerging technologies. This portfolio showcases both my professional experience and my continuous learning journey in data engineering.
